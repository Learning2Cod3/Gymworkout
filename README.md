# Comeback 12

A mobile-first 12-week workout + fat-loss tracker.

## What it does
- 12-week progressive training plan
- Workout logging for weight/reps/sets
- Weekly step and cardio targets
- Weight / waist / protein / steps check-ins
- Saves data locally on your phone/browser
- Installable as a Progressive Web App (PWA)
- Offline caching after first successful load

## Starting targets
- Protein: 190 g/day
- Calories: 2,600/day as an initial estimate
- Steps: ramps from 6,000/day to 9,000/day
- Rate of loss target: about 0.5–1.0% of body weight per week, but slower is fine if strength and waist improve

## Adjustment rule
After 2 full weeks, compare average morning body weight:
- Losing >2 lb/week and gym performance/recovery is suffering: add ~150–250 kcal/day.
- Losing ~0.5–2 lb/week: keep calories the same.
- No downward trend for 2–3 weeks with good adherence: subtract ~150–200 kcal/day or add modest activity.

## Safety
This is a general fitness planner, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Returning after a long layoff warrants conservative loading. Stop and seek medical evaluation for concerning symptoms such as chest pain/pressure, fainting, severe unexplained shortness of breath, or acute neurological symptoms.
